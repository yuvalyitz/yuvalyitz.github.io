This is Yuval Itzhaki’s personal academic website. For questions about this site or your information, [email me](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}).

### Hosting and external resources

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes request information when serving the site; see [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Some pages load scripts or styles from jsDelivr and Cloudflare’s cdnjs. Your browser sends these services request information, including your IP address, when fetching those resources. Links to other websites are governed by those sites’ own privacy practices.

### Analytics

The published site uses Google Analytics to measure visits and page usage. Google Analytics may store cookies and receive information about your browser and interactions with this site. See [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its [Analytics opt-out tool](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Preferences and contact

The light/dark theme switch saves your selection in your browser’s local storage. You can remove it by clearing this site’s stored browser data.

This site has no visitor accounts, public comment form, or advertising. Email links open your email application; if you write to me, I receive the message and the contact information you include so I can respond.
